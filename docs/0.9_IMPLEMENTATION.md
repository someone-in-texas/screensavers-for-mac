# 0.9 implementation

Version 0.9.0 was published on October 15, 2026. It adds Years in Bloom to the
standard pack and Commonplace to a separate AI pack. The review and validation
notes below describe the work leading to that release.

## Years in Bloom

Persistent, seeded `BloomStructure` geometry is separate from the continuous
`BloomCalendar` and retained Core Animation renderer. Stems extend with stroke
reveals, forks emerge at recorded birth ages, individual leaves unfold and fall,
and flowers give way to seed heads or fruit. Five-year cohorts retain woody growth;
annuals reseed with a different identity each year. A successor seedling bridges
composition renewal. Active monotonic time pauses while the saver is stopped.

The eighteen archetypes are oak, maple, birch, willow, pine, spruce, cedar, dogwood,
Japanese maple, sunflower, lavender, coneflower, wild grass, fern, rose, wildflowers,
sage and fruit tree. Their structures include recursive woody forks, narrow birch,
drooping willow terminals, evergreen sprays, arching fern fronds, herbaceous stems,
spike flowers, lobed/palmate leaves, ray flowers, seed plumes and seasonal fruit.
These are botanical illustrations, not scientific growth models.

Settings select a species or automatic mix; specimen/grouping/meadow/woodland or
automatic composition; Botanical, Vintage, Moonlit, Golden, Pastel, Ink or Vesper;
100/65/40 seconds per year; Calm/Natural/Dramatic weather; three paper textures;
and a season emphasis that lengthens rather than freezes the selected season.

Seeded weather combines evolving wind and quiet intervals, illustrated rain,
restrained distant storm illumination, seasonal paper washes, falling leaves,
winter snow and branch caps. Geometry is reused between frames. Cached paper and
bounded retained layers avoid geometry or texture generation on every frame.

## Commonplace

Commonplace renders cached local content into measured page layouts, then animates
physical sheets using three presentation layers. It uses eighteen layout families,
retina-resolution page caches, six theme-specific material systems and three density
levels plus automatic selection. Sepia Archive, Naturalist, Midnight Observatory,
Workshop, Atomic Lab and Off-World have distinct paper, typography and compositions.
Automatic theme selection holds its atmosphere for about 25 minutes.

Daily content is independent of presentation. Five opening days are committed as
one validated atomic batch; subsequent generation adds only a missing current day.
The optional helper holds a cross-process lease throughout generation. It uses the
existing managed ChatGPT sign-in through local Codex app-server, isolated credentials
and tool-free ephemeral threads. The saver contains no auth or generation code.
See [the helper contract](AI_HELPER.md) for setup, privacy and account limitations.

The helper sends a bounded recent ledger, unresolved questions, older fragments,
selected interests and dates. Replies must reference known entry IDs. A repeated-text
score rejects responses dominated by recent verbatim notes. Candidate searches never
become verified historical claims. Model content remains labeled fictional.

The archive preserves all daily batches while sampling recent and older material for
bounded playback. Variable-length chapters, entry/layout/image/pair cooldowns,
probabilistic quotation selection and per-display shuffle seeds reduce repetition.
Playback history stores identifiers only and merges under a short filesystem lock.
A confirmed reset preserves the old lineage. The helper exports source/day metadata.

Ten museum-verified CC0 starter images and three reviewed public-domain literary
extracts accompany the offline notebook. Optional daily retrieval uses the Cleveland
and Art Institute of Chicago APIs, per-item rights checks, provider URL restrictions,
conservative subject filters and bounded image downloads. Full source metadata is
retained when old derivative media is evicted. Original diagrams and notebook
measurement tables are fictional studies, distinct from verified museum metadata.

Options expose theme, pace, density, original writing, historical imagery, diagrams,
verified quotations and interests, plus explicit helper setup and source credits.
Generation, account state, reset, automatic daily availability and login-item opt-in
belong to the helper. Neither installer signs in or launches it.

## Five art review and revision cycles

Each numbered round used screenshots from the actual renderer, followed by changes
and fresh renders. Reviews are sampled sequences, not claims of physical-display
continuous playback or measured energy use.

| Round | Years in Bloom revisions | Commonplace revisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fit actual geometry bounds into the central composition; raise small specimens; add quiet ground washes. | Repair blank transition geometry; introduce irregular paper, material shadows, real plates and distinct page layouts. |
| 2 | Replace fishbone branches with curved recursive forks; add lobed oak/palmate maple leaves, arching fern tips and anatomical flower details. | Measure prose before placement; resize sparse cards; enlarge archival objects; preserve full source credit outside shortened captions. |
| 3 | Add seasonal paper atmosphere, retained winter evergreen pigment, snow caps, early foliage, staggered falling leaves and independent motion. | Replace overworked prose with ordinary observations; improve subject matching, chapter continuity, callbacks and heading clearance. |
| 4 | Slow annual structural growth; retain bent dry stems and seed heads; vary annual identities and bridge cohort renewal with seedlings. | Give themes different material/typographic systems, including Observatory slips/sky studies, workshop tracing, laboratory rulings and an ordinary human Off-World notebook. |
| 5 | Attach seed heads to the exact bent stem; share successor root anchors; stagger evergreen tiers and finish leaders; vary fern/lavender forms; add raised coneflower centers, drooping rays, pigment flecks and scattered leaf litter. | Reserve heading/prose/image-credit regions; measure all card content; vary theme grammars, chapter lengths, quotations and physical transitions; cap ultrawide sheet aspect; align captions to image edges; add fibrous slips and annotated original studies; remove empty decorative labels. |

## Validation and remaining manual checks

The offline archive simulations cover 5, 30, 180 and 365 seeded days, with accelerated
110-page runs exceeding two hours of logical playback. Repeated fixture prose tests
archive/render mechanics; it is not evidence of authenticated model-writing quality.
The archive logic test separately preserves 370 days through a full year of commits.

Final build/test/package evidence is recorded in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).
Reproduce galleries after `Scripts/build-preview.sh` with `build/preview-09
--bloom-gallery` or `--commonplace-gallery`, using `--output DIRECTORY`. Filters
include `--filter SPECIES_OR_THEME`, `--width`, `--height`, `--seed`, Bloom's
`--palette`/`--layout`, and Commonplace's `--pages`/`--archive-days`/`--dense`.
Archive-day simulations create and remove temporary fixtures; they do not modify
the user's notebook or contact a model. Native previews use `--bloom-review` or
`--commonplace-review --offline --profile` with isolated preference suites.
Final reviewed screenshots: [botanical study](images/years-in-bloom.png),
[winter evergreen](images/years-in-bloom-winter.png), [Sepia notebook](images/commonplace.png),
[ultrawide sheet](images/commonplace-wide.png), [Observatory](images/commonplace-observatory.png),
and [Off-World](images/commonplace-offworld.png).

Interactive ChatGPT browser sign-in, a paid/authenticated generation, clean-Mac
login-item approval and a physical hours-long screensaver soak remain manual checks.
Offline mocks and the isolated signed-out protocol smoke must not be described as
those tests. The local packages use ad-hoc signatures, not notarization.

The procedural botanical models intentionally share drawing primitives, and the
curated source library is modest. Museum metadata filtering is conservative, not a
complete image classifier. The three literary extracts are a reviewed seed catalog;
new AI search suggestions are not automatically promoted to verified quotations.
All 0.9 work remains local pending these acceptance checks and release authorization.
