# Art and internet mythology

Strawberry Fields Forever and Good Research Takes Time are original, fictional
worlds inspired by a particular corner of AI internet culture. Their imagery and
occasional text are not evidence about real research, private facilities, upcoming
models, capabilities, or release dates. Neither saver is affiliated with the people,
accounts, projects, or companies that inspired the references.

## Strawberry Fields Forever

A moonlit field grows above an underground research complex. Plant crowns continue
into roots, roots meet cable trunks, and a slow camera reveals the same physical
cross-section rather than cutting to an unrelated room. Dark red fruit, rounded
trifoliate leaves, flowers, atmospheric hills, a hatch and a warm fruit-inspection
bench carry the scene before any text appears.

Seeded terrain, plant placement, sky, connected cable routes and camera phase make
the field repeatable. Layered wind carries soft acceleration and deceleration
fronts. Signals travel along existing infrastructure. The basement's intermittent
readiness notices sometimes contradict earlier notices: this is the fictional
world's uncertainty, not an allegation about an actual person.

- **Pure Art:** no explicit lore text or deliberately staged three-fruit/basket
  arrangement. The field and underground computation remain.
- **Knowing** (default): one seed-selected triad, in the field or at the inspection
  bench, and restrained fictional research notices; no deep quotations.
- **Terminally Online:** very rare short cultural references, separated by long
  quiet intervals. Text None overrides every text mode.

Options include seven palettes, field/basement balance, three camera speeds, hype
weather, network visibility, wind, fruit density, text frequency and session/daily/
fixed seeds. No music, lyrics, profile images, album art, film imagery, social-media
interface, logos, or alleged proprietary architecture is included.

## Good Research Takes Time

A slow journey through an underground institution: a work desk and original maze
model, a chair alcove, and an archive observatory, connected by continuous stone
floors and distant passages. Paper, muted displays, an analog timer and localized
lamp light make the activity legible without scrolling terminal noise.

Seeded chamber dimensions, geology, archive details and original perfect mazes
vary the cave. The maze cursor follows an actual depth-first traversal, including
backtracking and long pauses. Experiment durations are fictional metadata; they
advance with the saver, not with a remote service. An almost-complete instrument
bar slowly reassesses itself without announcing a real release.

- **Pure Art:** ordinary research furniture, mazes and neutral experiment metadata;
  no explicit lore or staged chair shrine. A paper study occupies the alcove.
- **Subtle** (default): dignified chair framing and rare original patience notices.
- **Deep Lore:** optional rare insider phrases when Text is set to Lore. Messages
  wait for an instrument-panel camera visit, rather than multiplying across rooms.

Options include six palettes, three camera speeds, cave/archive/deep-lab/random
architecture, research activity, maze presence, text and session/daily/fixed seeds.
Daily seeds in both savers use the UTC date. Neither saver has network access,
analytics, runtime AI calls, social-media scraping, or sound.

## Grounding and source limits

The research was used to choose motifs, not to import posts or verify rumors.

- The [MazeBench organization](https://github.com/mazebench) links the patience-cave
  persona. The [official engine](https://github.com/mazebench/MazeBenchEngine) and
  [project site](https://mazebench.com/) support the visual-spatial reasoning and
  maze-evaluation connection. The saver's maze generator and artwork are original;
  no MazeBench assets are bundled.
- The [indexed patience-cave profile](https://x.com/patience_cave/with_replies)
  supports the chair, cave, waiting and research-time motifs. Direct X access was
  restricted during research; the saver has no live dependency on the account.
- A [contemporary X quotation](https://x.com/popular_12345/status/1822311743047012699)
  preserves the strawberry persona's triple-fruit identity and anticipation of a
  “big week.” This establishes a cultural motif, not insider knowledge.
- Direct access to the [older strawberry post](https://x.com/iruletheworldmo/status/1822364945226371306)
  was blocked. Short Ilya / “don't die” references are treated as user-requested
  internet mythology, not authenticated factual assertions. Basement, pacing and
  disappearing-note imagery are fictional artistic devices from the design brief.

Artistry takes priority over literal reference coverage. The absence of a particular
joke is intentional when it would weaken the landscape or the cave.
